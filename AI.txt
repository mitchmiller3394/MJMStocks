MJMStocks — AI Contribution Log
================================

Purpose
-------
This file tracks AI involvement over time, including:
1) what AI helped produce,
2) an approximate cumulative percentage of AI-generated code/content,
3) what the human author (Mitch) did in each step.

Update Rules
------------
- Append a new entry for every meaningful AI-assisted change.
- Keep entries short, factual, and dated.
- Treat percentages as rough estimates, not exact measurements.
- Re-estimate cumulative AI percentage after major refactors or rewrites.

Scale (for estimates)
---------------------
- 0% = fully human-authored, no AI drafting.
- 50% = mixed authorship, substantial AI drafting + substantial human edits.
- 100% = fully AI-drafted with minimal/no human text/code edits.
